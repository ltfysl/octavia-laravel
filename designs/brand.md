# Brand

## Name
Octavia — the prompt laboratory.

## Positioning
"Fine-tuning, but for prompts." Users do not train models; they benchmark, evolve and version prompts until they perform. The product sits between a lab instrument and a workshop: scientific method applied to a craft that most people currently do by gut feeling.

## Voice
- **Confident, not loud.** We state what the engine does; we never oversell ("bombenfester Engine" energy, delivered soberly).
- **Evidence-first.** Claims are backed by numbers shown on screen. Marketing copy avoids unverifiable superlatives.
- **Plain language, both languages.** EN and DE copy use the same direct register. No jargon without explanation.

## Logo
Wordmark-only for now: an "O" set in Space Grotesk Bold, white on the violet-600 tile, followed by "Octavia" in display weight. The single-letter tile scales from favicon (32px) to marketing hero.

## What the brand must never look like
- Generic SaaS admin template (no default Tailwind grays-on-white everywhere).
- Crypto/AI hype gradient soup. Gradients are limited to one soft radial wash behind the landing hero.
- Playful rounded-everything. Radii stay ≤ 10px; corners are quiet, content is sharp.
